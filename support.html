About the game

Jelly Hop Pop is a single-player 3D jumping game. You are a little red jelly hopping across wobbly jelly blocks floating on a candy sea. Hold to charge, release to jump. Land in the center of a block for double points, and land on three blocks of the same color in a row to pop them all.

How to play

  • Touch and hold to charge your jump, then let go. The longer you hold, the farther you jump. Touch the left or right half of the screen to choose which way to jump.
  • Landing in the center ring doubles your points, and center landings in a row multiply them even more. Five center landings in a row start Jelly Fever.
  • Land on three blocks of the same color in a row and they pop. Popping blocks fills the level goals.
  • Each level has a limited number of moves. Clear the goals before you run out to win up to three stars.
  • Special blocks: rainbow, bomb, ice, spring, moving and golden rescue jellies each do something different.
  • Outside the levels you can build Jelly Town with your stars, unlock new jelly characters with coins, climb in Endless mode and play a new Daily Challenge every day.
  • Pause: tap the pause button at the top left. Sound, music and vibration can be switched there or in Settings on the home screen.

Questions

Is my progress saved? Yes, automatically, on your device only. There is no account and no cloud save, so progress does not move between devices.

How do I reset my progress? Delete the App and install it again. This erases all coins, stars, characters and Jelly Town and cannot be undone.

How do lives work? Losing a level costs one life. Lives refill over time (one every 20 minutes), and you can watch an optional video for one more life.

The game runs slowly or gets warm. Close other apps, and make sure Low Power Mode is off.

Are there ads? Only optional rewarded videos: you can watch one to revive after falling into the water, to get 5 more moves, to double the coins from a level, or to get one more life. Nothing plays unless you tap to watch.

How do I report an inappropriate ad? While the ad is playing, tap its info (i) or AdChoices icon to report it to Google. You can also email contact@eastbigtech.com with a short description of the ad and, if you can, a screenshot.

Does the App collect my data? Your game progress stays on your device and we never receive it. The ads are provided by Google AdMob, which collects some device and ad information; if you allow tracking it may also use your advertising identifier. You can change tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
